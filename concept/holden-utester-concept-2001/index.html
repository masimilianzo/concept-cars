UTEster Concept

The Holden UTEster is a concept car produced by Holden, unveiled at the 2001 Melbourne Motor Show. Holden worked in conjunction with Car Tech and Paratus to create the UTEster. It was a convertible (targa top) coupé utility model based on the Holden Ute.
