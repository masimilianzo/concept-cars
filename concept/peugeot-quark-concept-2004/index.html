Quark Concept

The Peugeot Quark was a concept car from Peugeot, similar to a four-wheeled motorcycle or a quad bike. The Quark was unveiled at the 2004 Paris Motor Show and subsequently shown at the 2005 Amsterdam Motor Show. It utilised hydrogen fuel cells and had an electric motor on each wheel.
