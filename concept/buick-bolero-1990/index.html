Bolero

The Bolero was a concept car showcased by Buick in 1990. Displayed at the Detroit Auto Show in January 1990 and the Chicago Auto Show in February 1990, the Bolero served to preview numerous styling elements of the 1992 Buick Skylark. The Bolero's styling featured a prominent chromed feature line that moved upward along each side, wrapping around the rear deck, which was slightly higher than the hood.
