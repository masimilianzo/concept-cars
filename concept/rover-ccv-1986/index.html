Rover · 1986

CCV

The Rover CCV is a concept car shown by the British car company Austin Rover Group at the Turin Motor Show 1986 under the Rover marque. The CCV name stands for Coupé Concept Vehicle. Designed by Roy Axe, it followed similar design themes of the 1985 MG EX-E.
