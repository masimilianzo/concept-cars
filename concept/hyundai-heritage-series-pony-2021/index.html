Heritage Series Pony

A first-generation 1975 Pony disassembled and rebuilt with an electric powertrain, shown in April 2021 at Hyundai Motorstudio Busan. It keeps Giugiaro's original silhouette and adds camera-based mirrors, pixelated headlights and a speedometer made of lit vacuum tubes. It was the first car in Hyundai's Heritage Series.
