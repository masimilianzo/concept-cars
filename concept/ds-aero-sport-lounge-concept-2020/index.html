Aero Sport Lounge Concept

The DS Aero Sport Lounge is a Concept SUV made by DS Automobiles. Its design later inspired the design of later DS models, including the DS E-TENSE Performance concept in 2022. The Aero Sport Lounge was to make its debut at the 2020 Geneva International Motor Show, but due to the show's cancellation the concept never debuted and was subsequently never produced.
