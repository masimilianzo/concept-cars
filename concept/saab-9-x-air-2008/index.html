9-X Air

The Saab 9-X Air is a concept car created by Saab Automobile. It was first shown at the 2008 Paris Motor Show. It is a convertible version of the Saab 9-X Biohybrid, with a canvas roof referred to as the "Canopy Top" by Saab.
