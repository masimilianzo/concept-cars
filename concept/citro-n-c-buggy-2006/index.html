Citroën · 2006

C-Buggy

The Citroën C-Buggy was a concept car initially presented by Citroën at the 2006 editions of the Madrid Motor Show and the British International Motor Show. It was a two-seater city car with styling influences from both dune buggy and sport utility vehicle. It was made with some protection against off-road grievances, such as having a slightly raised suspension and also including sump guards.
