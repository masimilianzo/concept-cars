Nassau Concept

Nassau is the name used by several vehicles made by Chrysler. The name was first used as a trim level in 1955, denoting the least expensive two-door hardtop offered by Chrysler, which was part of the Chrysler Windsor series. More recently, two different concepts by the manufacturer bear the Nassau nameplate.
