Alfa Romeo · 1976

New York Taxi

The Alfa Romeo New York Taxi is a concept car designed by Italdesign in 1976 at the invitation of the New York Museum of Modern Art. The concept was designed to a brief from the museum aimed at producing a cleaner, more efficient, safer and more user friendly taxi. The New York Taxi was designed to be as compact as possible, while still being able to fit five passengers and baggage.
