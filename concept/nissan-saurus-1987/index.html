Saurus

The Nissan Saurus was a concept car designed by Nissan Motor and unveiled at the 1987 Tokyo Motor Show. It was a roadster featuring a 2.0-liter turbocharged engine, with bulging fenders and a design reminiscent of the Lotus Seven. Nissan marketed the Saurus as part of a pair along with the Jura minivan concept, introduced as a practical complement to the roadster.
