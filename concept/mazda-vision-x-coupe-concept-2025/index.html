VISION X-COUPE Concept

The Mazda Vision Coupe is a concept car developed by the Japanese car manufacturer Mazda, unveiled at the Tokyo Motor Show in 2017. It succeeds the Mazda RX-Vision concept and its design embodies Mazda's "Kodo" style. It takes the form of a long, four-door coupé, features a full panoramic roof, and previews future production models.
