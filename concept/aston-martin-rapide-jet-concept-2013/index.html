Aston Martin · 2013

Rapide Jet Concept

The Aston Martin Rapide Bertone Jet 2+2 is a one-off shooting brake designed by car enthusiast Barry Weir and the last ever vehicle to be built by car maker Bertone. It was shown at the 2013 Geneva Motor Show. The car was intended as a one-off special only but considering the positive response, Bertone decided to put the car into small scale production.
