Honda · 2015

Project 2&4

The Honda Project 2&4 was an open-wheel concept car that was initially unveiled at International Motor Show Germany in 2015. Its livery was largely inspired by the Honda RA272 Formula One race car, and the car itself was powered by the V4 engine derived from the Honda RC213V MotoGP motorcycle. The car was debuted at the 2015 Frankfurt Motor Show.
