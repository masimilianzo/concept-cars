Dodge · 2008

Zeo Concept

The Dodge ZEO (Zero Emissions Operation) was a concept car built by Dodge. The ZEO showcases that the electric powertrain technology will potentially faster than the gasoline powered muscle cars. The car was shown on 14 January 2008 at the 2008 North American International Auto Show.
