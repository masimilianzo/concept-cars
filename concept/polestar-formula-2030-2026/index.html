Polestar · 2026

Formula 2030 Close to production

A low, four-door electric sports car about 4.68 metres long, shown in Gothenburg on 10 November 2026 as the first show car from head of design Philipp Römers. It has Dual Blade headlights set into an aero wing, a shark nose, Single Blade rear lights, rear-hinged rear doors with no B-pillar, and a clamshell rear with a transparent floor. The cabin has a curved 20-inch driver display and eight reconfigurable physical controls. Polestar has not disclosed a powertrain.
