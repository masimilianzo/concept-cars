Argento Vivo

The Honda Argento Vivo was a concept car designed by Davide Arcangeli at Pininfarina studio under the Honda brand. It was presented at the 1995 Tokyo Motor Show. The very low silhouette of the car was finished with a front strip with narrow headlights, and the car body was made of aluminum and fiberglass.
