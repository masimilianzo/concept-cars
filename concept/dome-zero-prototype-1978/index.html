Dome · 1978

Zero Prototype

The Dome Zero (童夢-零, Dōmu Zero) was a prototype sports car from DOME Co. Ltd that was exhibited at the 48th Geneva Auto Show in 1978. The Dome project, pronounced "do-mu" or "child's dream" in Japanese, was started by Minoru Hayashi in 1975, with the goal of producing sports cars using knowledge gained from auto racing.
