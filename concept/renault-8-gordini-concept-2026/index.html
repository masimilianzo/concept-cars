8 Gordini Concept

A two-seat, rear-wheel-drive electric coupé that revives the 1960s Renault 8 Gordini, with a 270 hp motor, six round headlights and a Bleu de France paint colour. It was unveiled on 24 September 2026 and shown at the Paris Motor Show.
