Pontiac · 1954

Strato-Streak

The Pontiac Strato-Streak was a show car built by Pontiac for the 1954 General Motors Motorama. Its design was inspired by Pontiac's Catalina and based mechanically on the Star Chief's underpinnings. It had a 124 in (3,149.6 mm) wheelbase and was 54.7 in (1,389.4 mm) high.
