B6 Venator Concept

The Spyker B6 Venator is a concept car built by Dutch manufacturer Spyker Cars and first shown to the public at the Geneva Motor Show in April 2013. It was planned for production by 2014. The mid engined, rear-wheel drive concept is powered by a yet-to-be specified V6 engine, producing 375 bhp (280 kW; 380 PS), mated to a six-speed automatic.
