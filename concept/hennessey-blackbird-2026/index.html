Hennessey · 2026

Blackbird

Hennessey's third hypercar after the Venom GT and Venom F5, revealed during Monterey Car Week in August 2026. It takes its name and proportions from the Lockheed SR-71 and focuses on driver engagement: a naturally aspirated 6.2-litre Ilmor V8 with about 850 hp, a six-speed manual gearbox with an open-gate shifter, rear-wheel drive and a carbon-fibre body.
