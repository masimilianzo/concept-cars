Pu+Ra HPE

The Lancia Pu+Ra HPE is a concept car revealed on April 15, 2023. It was meant to showcase the styling and technology of future production models, including the all new Ypsilon. Pu+Ra refers to the brand's new "pure and radical" design language, while HPE stands for "High Performance Electric" and commemorates the badge used by the Lancia Beta shooting brake.
