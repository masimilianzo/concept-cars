Concept You

The Volvo Concept You is a concept car unveiled by Volvo at the 2011 Shanghai Auto Show. The Concept You is an executive car with a four-door fastback saloon styling and is a development of the Volvo Concept Universe, which was revealed earlier in 2011. The design builds upon Scandinavian Design.
