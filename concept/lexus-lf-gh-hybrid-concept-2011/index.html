LF-Gh Hybrid Concept

The Lexus LF-Gh ("Lexus Future Grand-touring Hybrid") is an LF concept vehicle by Lexus which debuted at the 2011 New York International Auto Show. The LF-Gh is a rear-wheel-drive hybrid platform powered by a Lexus Hybrid Drive powertrain. The LF-Gh also provide a hint of what future Lexus models, mainly the Lexus GS which was scheduled for late 2011, might look like.
