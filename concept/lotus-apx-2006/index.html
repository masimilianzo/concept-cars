Lotus · 2006

APX

The Lotus APX (standing for Aluminium Performance Crossover) is a concept car created by the British sports car marque, Lotus. It was first introduced at the 2006 Geneva Motor Show, and was also displayed as the Proton MSX. It is the first complete vehicle built on the new Versatile Vehicle Architecture (VVA).
