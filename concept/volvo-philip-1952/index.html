Volvo · 1952

Philip

The Volvo Philip was a concept car built by Volvo in 1952. It was designed especially for the United States market, and so was fitted with a prototype V8 engine called the B8B, which produced 120 hp (89 kW) at 4000 rpm along with whitewall tires and a hint of tailfins. The design was inspired by American cars and was similar to the 1951 Kaiser.
