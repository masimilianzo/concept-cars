Advanced Lightweight Coupe Concept

The Jaguar Advanced Lightweight Coupe (ALC) is a sports coupé concept car first shown at the 2005 North American International Auto Show in Detroit by the British automobile manufacturer Jaguar, then part of Ford's Premier Automotive Group. Designed by Ian Callum and Giles Taylor the concept previewed the Jaguar XK which was subsequently launched at the autumn 2005 Frankfurt Motor Show. The designers sought their inspiration from famous Jaguar automobiles of the past when designing the car, with the E-Type being the main inspiration as the grille of the ALC paid homage to the car.
