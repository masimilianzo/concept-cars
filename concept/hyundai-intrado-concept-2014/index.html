Intrado Concept

The Hyundai Intrado is a mini SUV concept car made by Korean company Hyundai. It is powered by a hydrogen fuel cell powertrain with a 36-kWH lithium-ion battery. It has a range of about 370 mi (600 km) on a single fill up.
