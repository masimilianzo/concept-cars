Citroën · 2001

Osée

The Citroën Osée is a concept car designed by Pininfarina for the French car company Citroën. It was unveiled at the 2001 Geneva Motor Show. The Osée is powered by a mid-mounted 3-litre V6 engine, providing the vehicle with 200 bhp (149 kW) at 6000 rpm, with a maximum torque of 267 N⋅m (197 lb⋅ft) at 3750 rpm.
