Eco 2000

The Citroen Eco 2000 was a project by Citroën that resulted series of concept cars to demonstrate the French car manufacturer's design and technology with aerodynamics and efficiency and started in 1981. The Eco 2000 name covers four distinct models which show the evolution of Citroën's thinking: In 1982 the SA 103, in 1983 the SA 117, and in March 1984 with the SA 109. Later in October 1984 came the fourth and final version known as the SL10.
