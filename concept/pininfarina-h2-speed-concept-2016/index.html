Pininfarina · 2016

H2 Speed Concept

The Pininfarina H2 Speed is a hydrogen-powered concept car developed by Pininfarina and GreenGT. The car was first presented in 2016. It notably appears in the racing game Asphalt Legends as a C-class car.
