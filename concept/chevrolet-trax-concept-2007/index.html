Chevrolet · 2007

Trax Concept

The Chevrolet Trax Concept was one of the three small car concepts introduced at the 2007 New York International Auto Show. The Trax showcased a new style of small car from General Motors with a two-tone exterior. The mini car was completely designed by GM Daewoo in South Korea.
