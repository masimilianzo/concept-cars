Citroën · 1980

Karin

The Citroën Karin is a concept car presented at the Paris Motor Show in 1980. It features a striking, pyramidal design by Trevor Fiore. The exterior of the car incorporated flush glass panels, faired rear wheels, and butterfly doors.
