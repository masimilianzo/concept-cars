YCC Concept

The Volvo YCC ("Your Concept Car") is a concept car made by Volvo Cars presented at the 2004 Geneva Motor Show, with the stated goal of meeting the particular needs of female drivers. In order to do so, Volvo assembled a design team entirely made up of women, around October 2001. It was an exercise in ergonomics from the perspective of a female driver.
