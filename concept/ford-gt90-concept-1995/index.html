Ford · 1995

GT90 Concept

The Ford GT90 is a concept car that was developed and manufactured by American car maker Ford. It was unveiled in January 1995 at the Detroit Auto Show. As of 2025, the car is owned by Hajek Motorsports Museum, Ames, Oklahoma.
