Bevel Concept

The Nissan Bevel was a concept crossover SUV unveiled at the 2007 North American International Auto Show. It was of asymmetrical design, with regular front and rear doors on the passenger side, but just one large driver's side door. Carlos Tavares, Nissan Motor Co.
