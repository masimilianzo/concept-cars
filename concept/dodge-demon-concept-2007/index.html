Demon Concept

The Dodge Demon was a roadster concept car made by DaimlerChrysler, designed to be slotted below the Viper for the Dodge brand. The name was previously used in the 1970s for the Dodge Dart Demon, a fastback model. The Demon was first shown at the 2007 Geneva Auto Show, and was considered for production.
