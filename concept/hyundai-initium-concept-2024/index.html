INITIUM Concept

The Hyundai Initium (Korean: 현대 이니시움) is a concept model of a fuel cell electric vehicle by Hyundai. It was unveiled in Goyang, South Korea in October 2024 and at the 2024 LA Auto Show. The production version of the concept model has been revealed as the second-generation Hyundai Nexo.
