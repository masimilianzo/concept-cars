Vision Gran Turismo

The Vision Gran Turismo Program (commonly abbreviated Vision GT or VGT) is a series of virtual concept cars developed by various car manufacturers for the Gran Turismo series of sim racing games. Debuting in Gran Turismo 6, Vision GT cars later made an appearance in each subsequent Gran Turismo installment (Gran Turismo Sport and Gran Turismo 7), all developed by Polyphony Digital. For the most part, they apply present-day technology and materials, with a handful of notable exceptions that utilize novel propulsion and aerodynamic technologies.
