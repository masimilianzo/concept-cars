Daihatsu · 2005

Costa

The Daihatsu Costa is a concept car introduced at the 2005 Tokyo Motor Show. It is basically a city car designed to be taken to the beach. The Costa weighs 1,654 pounds (750 kg) and has no doors, similar to a golf cart.
