McL 6GT Concept

A carbon-fibre tribute to the M6GT road car that Bruce McLaren was developing before his death in 1970. It pairs a V8 with rear-wheel drive and a manual gearbox, McLaren's first manual since the F1. It was revealed at Monterey Car Week 2026.
