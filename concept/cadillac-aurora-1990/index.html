Cadillac · 1990

Aurora

The Cadillac Aurora was a concept car manufactured by Cadillac; styled under the direction of GM Vice President of Design, Chuck Jordan; and presented at the 1990 Chicago Auto Show. After the Aurora failed to enter production, its nameplate, powertrain concept and interior design concept were subsequently used by the Oldsmobile Aurora, presented in 1995. Its exterior design carried styling cues of the European Opel Omega B saloon released in 1994.
