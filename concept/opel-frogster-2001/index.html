Frogster

The Opel Frogster is a concept car built by Opel in 2001. It premiered at the September 2001 Frankfurt Motor Show. The Frogster could be transformed from a convertible to a roadster or a pickup truck by using a built in personal digital assistant (PDA), which was mounted between the two front seats.
