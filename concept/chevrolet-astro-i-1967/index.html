Chevrolet · 1967

Astro I

The Chevrolet Astro I (XP-842) was a concept car created for 1967. The nose design of the Astro I was quite similar to the Mako Shark show car. It was previously called X-1000 Corvair Super GT Low Roof Aerodynamic Coupe.
