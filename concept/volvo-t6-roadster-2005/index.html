Volvo · 2005

T6 Roadster

The Volvo T6 was a concept car from Volvo presented in 2005 at SEMA. There was just one hand built copy, although there was some discussion about a limited production run. It is powered by a twin turbocharged 2.9 litre inline six cylinder engine from Volvo S80 giving a reported 300 bhp (224 kW) but with no official reported top speed. The engine is located behind the driver.
