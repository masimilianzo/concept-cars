Ford · 1999

021C

A styling exercise by the designer Marc Newson, built by Carrozzeria Ghia in Turin and shown at the October 1999 Tokyo Motor Show, where it won the Best Concept Car award. A compact four-door with a single horizontal LED headlight and tail light, swivelling seats, suicide doors, a floating instrument panel and a luggage compartment that opens like a drawer. Its name is linked to Pantone's 021C orange and, according to Ford, to the 21st century. It later appeared in lime green at the Milan furniture fair and is now in the Petersen Automotive Museum.
