GSX-R/4 Concept

The Suzuki GSX-R/4 is a concept car made by Suzuki in 2001. Designed as a showcase for technology, it was never meant for production. It has a mid-mounted, 1.3 L (79 in3) DOHC engine taken from the company's flagship motorcycle, the GSX-1300R Hayabusa with a top speed reaching of 181 mph (291 km/h) at 9800 rpm.
