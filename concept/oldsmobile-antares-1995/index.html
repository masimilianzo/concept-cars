Antares

The Oldsmobile Antares was a 1995 concept car built by Oldsmobile. The Antares's design was heavily based on Oldsmobile's new "Aurora-look", derived from the 1994 Oldsmobile Aurora. The Antares is named after a bright star in the Southern Hemisphere, a prominent star in the constellation Scorpius.
