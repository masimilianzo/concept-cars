Honda · 2005

WOW

The Honda WOW ("Wonderful Open-hearted Wagon") was a concept car created by the automobile division of Honda, first introduced at the 2005 Tokyo Motor Show. The WOW was designed to fit the needs of both a person and dogs. The WOW concept was built with a low center of gravity, giving it a more stable driving experience.
