Hyundai · 2021

Heritage Series Grandeur

An electric reinterpretation of the 1986 first-generation Grandeur, unveiled for the model's 35th anniversary as the second car in Hyundai's Heritage Series after the Pony EV concept. It keeps the boxy original silhouette and adds pixel-style LED lights, new mirrors and wheels. The cabin features a virtual piano developed with Samick and bronze-toned lighting. Hyundai has not disclosed powertrain details.
