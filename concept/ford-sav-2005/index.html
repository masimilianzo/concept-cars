Ford · 2005

SAV

The Ford SAV (an acronym for Sports Activity Vehicle, not to be confused with BMW's term for SUVs) was a seven-seater large MPV concept car developed by Ford Europe. It was first displayed to the public at the 2005 Geneva Motor Show and was intended as a styling exercise to show the new direction for Ford's designs in the European market. Ford introduced this model one year later as the Ford S-MAX with slight modifications for mass production.
