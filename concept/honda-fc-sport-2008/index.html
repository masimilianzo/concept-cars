Honda · 2008

FC Sport

The Honda FC Sport is a concept car that was produced by Honda, first shown at the 2008 Los Angeles Auto Show. It was described as a design study and was intended to show what a hydrogen powered sports car might look like. The FC Sport was designed for a drivetrain similar to the one found in the Honda FCX Clarity.
