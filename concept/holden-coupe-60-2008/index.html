Holden · 2008

Coupe 60

The Holden Coupe 60 is a concept sports coupe developed by Holden. It was unveiled at the 2008 Melbourne International Motor Show, alongside the HSV W427. It featured a pillarless design, and utilised Holden's rear-wheel drive Zeta platform.
