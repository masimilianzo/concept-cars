Alfa Romeo · 1997

Sportut

The Alfa Romeo Sportut is a concept car designed by Bertone for the Italian automobile manufacturer Alfa Romeo. It was based on an Alfa Romeo 145 platform and first shown at the 1997 Geneva Motor Show, as a non-moving styling exercise. The vehicle's body style is a five-door SUV, with hidden rear door-handles like the Alfa Romeo 156.
