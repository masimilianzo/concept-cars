Tesla · 2017

Roadster (prototype)

The second-generation Tesla Roadster, shown in red at the Tesla Semi unveiling in November 2017. Tesla claimed a four-seat electric supercar with a removable glass roof, 0 to 60 mph in 1.9 seconds, a top speed above 250 mph and a range of around 620 miles.

What it became

Tesla promised production in 2020 at a base price of about $200,000, but the programme has slipped repeatedly. Recent reports describe a redesigned Roadster with production now targeted for 2027 or later.
