Saab · 2008

9-X Biohybrid

The Saab 9-X Biohybrid concept car was developed by Saab in 2008, and first shown at the Geneva Motor Show. It is based on the GM Delta platform, and was thought to give an idea of the cancelled Saab 9-1X. It has keyless entry and small cameras instead of rear-view mirrors.
