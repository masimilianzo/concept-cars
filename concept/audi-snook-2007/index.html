Audi · 2007

Snook

The Audi Snook is the name given to a concept for a single person, single-wheel vehicle that is stabilized electronically. The vehicle has been shown at the following events or won the following awards: 2007 Frankfurt Motor Show, where it won the VDA Design Award. January 2008 Detroit Auto Show, where it won the Michelin Design Award.
