GS Camargue

The Citroën GS Camargue is a concept car based on the Citroën GS, presented as a three-door hatchback with 2+2 seating. It was designed by the Italian company Bertone and presented in 1972 at the Geneva Motor Show. The GS Camargue used GS mechanical components, and was the same overall length, but 6 cm (2.4 in) wider and 20 cm (7.9 in) lower.
