Egoista

The Lamborghini Egoista is a concept car unveiled by Lamborghini in 2013 for the company's 50th anniversary. The fully functioning model is powered by the 5.2 L (317 cu in) V10 engine from the Gallardo producing 600 hp (447 kW; 608 PS). The name "Egoista" comes from the Italian word for selfish.
