British Leyland · 1969

Zanda

The British Leyland Zanda Grand Turismo is a concept car first unveiled at the British Motor Show in 1969 on the Pressed Steel stand. It was not designed as a future model but to showcase the Austin Morris design capability and the technical capability of Pressed Steel. The car is non-running and is on show at the British Motor Museum in Gaydon.
