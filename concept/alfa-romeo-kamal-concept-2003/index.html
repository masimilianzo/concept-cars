Alfa Romeo · 2003

Kamal Concept

The Alfa Romeo Kamal is a crossover SUV (sports utility vehicle) concept car designed by the Italian car manufacturer Alfa Romeo, under the project code 921. The Kamal was presented at the 2003 Geneva Motor Show. The vehicle used the Alfa Romeo 147 platform, and was initially set for launch in 2007.
